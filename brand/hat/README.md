# Hat lockups

Small format, arrows, no crow, no tagline. Ready to upload to a hat designer.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `hat-arrows.svg` / `.png` | Dark ink, for a light hat |
| `hat-arrows-white.svg` / `.png` | Light ink, for a dark hat |
| `hat-arrows-stitch.svg` / `.png` | Stitch-safe version, dark ink |
| `hat-arrows-stitch-white.svg` / `.png` | Stitch-safe, light ink |

PNGs are 2712 px across with a transparent background, which is roughly 600 dpi
on a 4.5 in hat front. SVGs are vector and preferable wherever the vendor accepts
them.

## Which one to send

`hat-arrows` carries the traced public domain arrow, with its full fletching. It
is the better looking mark and the right choice for print, patches and anything
sized generously.

`hat-arrows-stitch` swaps in a plainer arrow. On the measurements the fletched
version leaves 19.7 percent of its ink in strokes too fine to embroider, against
6.9 percent for this one. If the vendor is stitching directly onto the cap rather
than printing a patch, send this.

If you are unsure, send both and ask which their machine prefers. A digitiser can
usually tell at a glance.

## Colour

Each mark is a single flat fill, so recolouring is one attribute in the SVG. The
two supplied colours are the site tokens: ink `#141210` and paper `#F2EADC`.
